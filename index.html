Arrow Maze Deluxe — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Version 24.2

This policy explains how the current Android test version of Arrow Maze Deluxe handles information. It applies to the Android game and its bundled offline puzzle interface. This policy must be reviewed and updated before any advertising, analytics, cloud-save or other third-party SDK is added.

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores puzzle progress, unlocked level number, stars, coin balance, current puzzle state, audio volume and ON/OFF preferences, and privacy preference choices in local app storage on your device. This information is used to save your game and settings. It is not sent to an Arrow Maze developer server by this test version.

2. Information not collected by the current test build

The current test build has no Arrow Maze account registration or sign-in. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or file access. It does not include an advertising SDK or analytics SDK and does not transmit gameplay progress or settings to an Arrow Maze developer server.

3. Google Play Billing

The Android project includes Google Play Billing support for a planned optional one-time Remove Ads product. The current test interface keeps the purchase action disabled until the product and advertising setup are completed. The app can communicate with Google Play Billing to check product availability and restore an existing purchase entitlement. If a purchase is enabled in a future release, Google Play processes the transaction and payment method. Google Play may process account and transaction information under Google's own privacy terms. The app receives limited product and purchase-status information needed to manage the entitlement; it does not receive the user's full payment card details.

4. Advertising and analytics

The current test build does not show advertisements and does not use analytics. The Privacy Preferences screen stores local choices for optional advertising and usage analytics; those choices do not activate or control an advertising or analytics service in this test build because no such SDK is included. If these services are added, this policy, the in-app consent flow and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be reviewed and updated before release.

5. Data retention and deletion

Game data remains in local app storage until the user selects Delete Local Game Data in the Privacy Rights screen, clears the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalls the app. There is no Arrow Maze account or cloud profile in this version. Google Play purchase records are handled by Google Play and are subject to Google's retention and account controls.

6. Children's privacy

The current build has no account creation, social profile or developer-operated collection of personal information. The app's target-audience and content-rating declarations will be completed accurately in Google Play Console before publication. If the app is configured for children or families, additional applicable requirements will be reviewed before release.

7. Security

Game progress and preferences are stored locally by the app. Google Play is responsible for its own payment processing and transaction security for purchases made through Google Play Billing.

8. Changes to this policy

If app features, advertising, analytics, cloud saving, permissions or third-party SDKs change, this policy will be updated to accurately describe those practices. The effective date above will also be updated.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact arrowmazedeluxe.support@gmail.com.
